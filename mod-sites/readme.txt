This mod allows you to have one backup/rollback for your singleplayer worlds, and you can create or restore them with a keybind or from the death screen at any point.

This can be useful for many cases where you want to play and fully restore adventure maps, parkour courses, hardcore maps, testing worlds, and any other types of maps at the click of a button without any system in the world itself, needed to any state you wish to back it up at.
